Site notes

Privacy and image use

How this personal photography archive treats visitor data and its photographs.

Privacy

This is Ricky Kwok’s personal, non-commercial photography archive. It has no accounts, forms or mailing lists, and it never asks for your name or email address.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics loads only if you choose “Allow analytics”. If you do, Google receives the page address (without query strings), the referring page address, your browser and device details and your IP address, plus three gallery actions: opening an artwork, filtering a series and switching language. Google may set its own analytics cookies. Advertising features and Google signals are switched off, and Google may process this data outside Canada.

If you decline, or make no choice, nothing is sent to Google.

Your choice

Your choice is kept in your browser’s local storage under the name “rickykwok.analytics-consent.v2”. You can change it at any time with “Analytics choices” at the foot of every page. Declining stops future measurement and removes Google Analytics cookies that this site can reach; you can also clear cookies in your browser settings.

Hosting

Pages are delivered through Cloudflare and GitHub Pages, which process IP addresses to deliver pages and keep the site secure. This site does not set any cookies of its own.

Image use

All photographs are © Ricky Kwok. Unless a photograph’s own page says otherwise, all rights are reserved and no reproduction is authorized through this website.

A small number of photographs are released under CC BY-SA 4.0; where that applies, the photograph’s own page states it and those terms govern that image. There is no permission or request process through this site.
