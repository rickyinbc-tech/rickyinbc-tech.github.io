Defining milestone · 2014

A photograph that stayed with me.

In 2014, while still at secondary school, Ricky Kwok won the Photo Competition—Secondary School Category of National Geographic Channel Hong Kong’s Nat Geo Awards with 整整有條.

It remains Ricky’s most meaningful photographic recognition. The artwork record preserves the photograph, exact result, and contemporary source.

Project note

Hong Kong light pollution, seen from street level.

Light Encroached Homes / 光染民居 was made in Mong Kok in 2014 and selected as champion from 154 entries in the University of Hong Kong’s Light Pollution Photography Competition.

The archive entry keeps the capture process, environmental context, judges’ response and Hong Kong Space Museum exhibition beside the photograph itself.

About

Hong Kong, through my lens.

I’m Ricky Kwok 郭文棣, also known as Kwok Man Tai. I photograph everyday life, light, and movement. This non-commercial archive gathers the photographs and their documented stories.
