The defining milestone · 2014

The achievement that means the most to Ricky.

With 整整有條, Ricky won the Photo Competition—Secondary School Category of National Geographic Channel Hong Kong’s Nat Geo Awards 2014.

A contemporaneous on.cc report documents the result; an organizer result page is not currently available. Ricky regards it as his most meaningful achievement as a photographer—a statement of personal significance, not an official ranking among competitions.

Evidence method

Strength is shown, not flattened.

“Publicly traceable” does not mean every result has the same level of proof.

ARPS means Associate of the Royal Photographic Society. It is a photographic distinction, not a competition placement, and is therefore outside the total of 31. Read the RPS definition.

A Direct result

The organizer or awarding body directly publishes the name and result.

B Institutional record

A school, university, contemporaneous report, or related institution clearly records the result.

C Partial support

Public evidence supports only part of the claim; the unresolved detail is stated.

D Legacy claim

Only an old self-description has been found. These claims are not included in the total.
