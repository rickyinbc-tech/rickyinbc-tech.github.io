The defining milestone · 2014

The achievement that means the most to Ricky.

With 整整有條, Ricky won the Photo Competition—Secondary School Category of National Geographic Channel Hong Kong’s Nat Geo Awards 2014.

Documented in contemporaneous 2014 coverage, it remains Ricky’s most meaningful achievement as a photographer and a defining image in his early practice.

Record overview · reviewed 15 August 2026

One person, five published name forms.

Ricky Kwok, 郭文棣, Kwok Man Tai, Kwok Man-tai, and Man Tai Kwok refer to the same photographer. HKU and City Gallery bridge the Chinese and romanized names; Sony uses Ricky Kwok, while FVM’s organizer archive uses Man Tai Kwok.

Published English titles are retained where available, while Chinese competition names are accompanied by clear descriptive translations.
