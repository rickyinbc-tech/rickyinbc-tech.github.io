Series Overview

Ritual begins where ordinary gestures become ceremonial.

Kwok approaches documentary photography as a form of theatre already present in daily life. Fishermen lift lamps on dark water; people gather around a hearth; smoke rises through old kitchens; elders and children share laughter outside village houses. The photographs are presented for visual study; staging, subject direction, access, and consent are not inferred and remain outside the archive’s documented record.

Ritual is rooted in Kwok’s attention to human rhythm: a hand extended toward fire, a face turned toward light, and a small community held together by gesture. Work, memory, and performance remain visible in the same frame.
