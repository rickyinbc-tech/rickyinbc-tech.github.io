Series Overview

Motion is where the body leaves a trace of itself.

Kwok treats sport as a language of line, pressure, and repetition. Swimmers become bands of turquoise, divers form descending sequences, and horses compress into a charging mass. He looks for the instant when recognizable action begins to dissolve into abstraction while retaining the emotional charge of competition or performance.

Blur is not a loss of information here; it is the subject. These photographs hold rhythm, strain and acceleration as visible material. The body remains present as a streak, a wake, a pulse or a curve through water and light.
