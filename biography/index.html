Biography

Ricky Kwok 郭文棣, ARPS

A Hong Kong photography enthusiast working between documentary observation and controlled photographic experiments.

ARPS: Associate of The Royal Photographic Society.

Personal Photography Notes

I study the moment when ordinary reality becomes unstable.

A river lamp becomes a stage light, a swimmer dissolves into colour, an urban signboard becomes environmental pressure, and a droplet becomes sculpture.

Moving between documentary observation and controlled photographic experiments, I treat time as physical material. The images are rooted in Hong Kong’s density, ritual life, and visual intensity, while extending into high-speed studies of water, ash, powder, and motion.

Bio

Hong Kong, light, ritual, and impact.

Ricky Kwok, also known as Kwok Man Tai and 郭文棣, is a Hong Kong photography enthusiast and an Associate of the Royal Photographic Society.

Practice

Kwok’s photographic work moves through ritual scenes, Hong Kong urban night, sport abstraction, and controlled high-speed studies. The images often turn a brief instant into a visual object.

Training Record

Public records identify Kwok Man Tai as a CCC Chuen Yuen College student at the time of the 2014 HKU light pollution award, and later as an HKMU Cinematic, Design & Photographic Digital Art student in 2016.

Recognition

Selected public records include Commended, Youth, Culture at the 2015 Sony World Photography Awards; Champion of HKU’s 2014 Light Pollution Photography Competition for Light Encroached Homes / 光染民居; four student-category placements in the Hong Kong Bird Watching Society fishpond competition; and Outstanding Award in the Secondary School Section of the 2013 「城市印象＠你我社區」Photography Competition. Early school-era records may use 郭文棣 or Kwok Man Tai.

Distinction

ARPS means Associate of the Royal Photographic Society, an RPS distinction. The Royal Photographic Society’s Associate page defines the designation.

Ricky Kwok (Kwok Man Tai / 郭文棣), ARPS, is a Hong Kong photography enthusiast whose recognition spans documentary, environmental, and urban photography. Selected results include Commended, Youth, Culture at the 2015 Sony World Photography Awards; Champion of the University of Hong Kong’s 2014 Light Pollution Photography Competition for Light Encroached Homes / 光染民居; four student-category landscape and ecology placements in the Hong Kong Bird Watching Society’s 2014 fishpond competition; Outstanding Award in the Secondary School Section of the 2013 「城市印象＠你我社區」Photography Competition; and Merit Prize in the 2016 Equal Opportunities Photo Competition. National Geographic Channel’s 2014 Nat Geo Awards reported 整整有條 as Secondary-school Category Champion in a contemporaneous result report.
