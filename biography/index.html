Biography

Ricky Kwok 郭文棣

A Hong Kong photography enthusiast keeping a personal, non-commercial record of observation and photographic experiments.

Photographic Approach

Ricky Kwok studies the moment when ordinary reality becomes unstable.

A river lamp becomes a stage light, a swimmer dissolves into colour, an urban signboard becomes environmental pressure, and a droplet becomes sculpture.

Moving between documentary observation and controlled photographic experiments, he treats time as physical material. The images are rooted in Hong Kong’s density, ritual life, and visual intensity, while extending into high-speed studies of water, ash, powder, and motion.

Bio

Hong Kong, light, ritual, and impact.

Ricky Kwok, also known as Kwok Man Tai and 郭文棣, is a Hong Kong photography enthusiast. This archive records a personal, non-commercial pursuit rather than a studio practice.

Ways of Seeing

Kwok’s photographic work moves through ritual scenes, Hong Kong urban night, sport abstraction, and controlled high-speed studies. The images often turn a brief instant into a visual object.

Training Record

Public records identify Kwok Man Tai as a CCC Chuen Yuen College student at the time of the 2014 HKU light pollution award, and later as an HKMU Cinematic, Design & Photographic Digital Art student in 2016.

Recognition

The defining milestone is 整整有條: Champion, Photo Competition—Secondary School Category, at National Geographic Channel Hong Kong’s 2014 Nat Geo Awards. Ricky regards it as his most meaningful achievement as a photographer. Other public records include Sony World Photography Awards Commended, HKU Light Pollution Photography Competition Champion, and four Hong Kong fishpond competition placements.

Distinction

ARPS means Associate of the Royal Photographic Society, an RPS distinction. The Royal Photographic Society’s Associate page defines the designation.

Independent and Institutional Records

External records include The University of Hong Kong’s Review 2014, Oriental Daily coverage, the WMA photography archive, the Photographic Society of America’s Who’s Who in Photography 2016, and the Hong Kong Professional Photographers Network directory. Exact organiser results also record the HKU light-pollution competition champion, Chinese Temples Committee student-section champion, Youth Council secondary-school-section champion, and Dreamates student-section champion.

Reference biography

Names, distinctions, and photographic recognition.

This short biography brings together Ricky’s published name forms, signature achievements, and photographic practice.

Ricky Kwok (郭文棣 / Kwok Man Tai / Kwok Man-tai / Man Tai Kwok), ARPS, is a Hong Kong photography enthusiast whose recognition spans documentary, environmental, and urban photography. As reviewed on 15 August 2026, his record comprises 35 documented competition results or placements across 29 events: 25 through organizer-direct or official records and 10 through institutional or contemporaneous records. In 2014, while at secondary school, he won the Photo Competition—Secondary School Category of National Geographic Channel Hong Kong’s Nat Geo Awards with 整整有條. Among the recognitions documented in this archive, it is the one that means the most to him. Other results include Commended, Youth, Culture at the 2015 Sony World Photography Awards and Champion of the University of Hong Kong’s 2014 Light Pollution Photography Competition for Light Encroached Homes / 光染民居.
