Awarded photograph · 2014

整整有條

Repetition, labour, and a single human figure turn an industrial worksite into a precise monochrome field.

Artwork & award record

整整有條

  • Documented title: 整整有條
  • Archive English title: Coil Field
  • Series: Ritual
  • Recognition: Champion, Photo Competition—Secondary School Category, Nat Geo Awards 2014
  • Organizer: National Geographic Channel Hong Kong
  • Photographer: Ricky Kwok / 郭文棣 / Kwok Man Tai
  • Artwork type: Photograph
  • Copyright: © Ricky Kwok. All rights reserved. No reproduction is authorized through this website.

Rows of circular coils fill the frame while a worker bends within the pattern. The monochrome treatment concentrates attention on shape, scale, and labour: the geometry remains inseparable from the person making and tending it.

Entered under its documented title, 整整有條, the photograph won the Photo Competition—Secondary School Category of National Geographic Channel Hong Kong’s 2014 Nat Geo Awards. Among the recognitions documented in this archive, it is the achievement that means the most to Ricky as a photographer.

Award record: The photograph shown here appears in the contemporaneous on.cc result report dated 28 June 2014, documenting Ricky’s Secondary School Category championship.
